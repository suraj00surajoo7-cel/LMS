Career in AI & Machine Learning

A staged path from Python and mathematics to ML, deep learning, NLP, computer vision, Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, and AI agent projects.

4learning stages6capstone ideas
Stage 1

Python, Math & Statistics

  • Python, NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib, Jupyter, Git, and clean notebook habits.
  • Linear algebra, probability, descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, and calculus intuition.
  • Project: exploratory data analysis with a clear written insight report.
Stage 2

Machine Learning

  • Regression, classification, clustering, recommendation systems, and anomaly detection.
  • Feature engineering, train/test split, cross-validation, model metrics, and bias checks.
  • Project: student performance predictor or spam email classifier.
Stage 3

Deep Learning Domains

  • Neural networks, CNNs, transfer learning, RNNs, transformers, and model tuning.
  • NLP, computer vision, reinforcement learning concepts, and dataset preparation.
  • Project: face attendance system, plant disease detector, or sentiment dashboard.
Stage 4

Generative AI & Production

  • LLMs, prompt engineering, embeddings, vector databases, RAG, LangChain, and agents.
  • Hugging Face, TensorFlow, PyTorch, FastAPI, Streamlit, MLflow, and deployment basics.
  • Project: college admission chatbot or document Q&A assistant with responsible AI notes.
